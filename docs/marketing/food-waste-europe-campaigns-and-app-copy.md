# FreeToGo — European food-waste research, brand messaging and app copy

Status: **Marketing and product copy draft, 2026-10-14**. Not a production-approved statistical dataset. Local-language copy requires native-speaker review; statistical figures and definitions require final primary-source validation prior to publication.

## Product and messaging principles

FreeToGo is an **entirely free** surplus-food sharing app for pickup customers and merchants. It supports (1) free reserved food bags and (2) open, non-reserved food distributions with clear uncertainty about stock. Users can subscribe to notifications from favorite stores, individual stores, local area and selected towns. Keep the app minimalist. The primary promise should be the practical benefit and optimistic community impact, not guilt or institutional environmental messaging.

Brand lines:
- **Good food. For free.** (primary international line)

- **Too good to waste.**
- **Free food is closer than you think.**

## Methodological warning: total food waste ≠ edible food waste

**Food waste / elintarvikejäte** often includes inedible parts and different food-chain stages. Do **not** describe all these amounts as edible food, food recoverable by FreeToGo, or food which would otherwise definitely be thrown away. National definitions, reference years and coverage may differ.

### EU and world campaign figures (research-stage)

- EU in 2024: about **57.9 million tonnes** of food waste/year, **129 kg/person/year**; household waste approx. **31 million tonnes**. Source to reconfirm: Eurostat, food waste statistics, latest 2024 edition (publication 29 Sept 2026 per prior research).
- EU food retail plus restaurants/food services: approx. **11.2 million tonnes/year** (food waste, not all edible or recoverable). Source: Eurostat, verify definitions.
- World in 2022: **1.05 billion tonnes** of food waste, including inedible parts. UNEP Food Waste Index Report 2024. UNEP also estimates **more than one billion meals wasted per day by households**; avoid implying FreeToGo can recover all of these.
- Finnish original background reported ~600–640 million kg total food waste; ~360 million kg edible food waste; households >100 million kg edible waste; older working estimates ~100–120 kg/person total, ~60–70 kg/person edible. These figures refer to different indicators/years: do not mix with Eurostat totals. Prior research referred to Luke's **2023: 641 million kg food waste and 369 million kg edible food waste**; **2024 Eurostat: around 585 million kg all food waste**, and a Luke statement that households represented around 57% of all Finnish food waste in 2024. All figures need primary-source cross-check before publishing.
- Avoid the unqualified original assertion that Finnish households account for 30–40% of **all** food waste, since definitions differ.
- Common household wasted categories reported in the initial brief: vegetables, root vegetables, fruits and coffee. Validate category ranking before publication.
- Suggested sources: [Eurostat Food Waste](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php?title=Food_waste_and_food_waste_prevention_-_estimates); [UNEP Food Waste Index](https://www.unep.org/resources/global-waste-management-outlook-2024) (use actual Food Waste Index Report 2024 page when finalizing); [Luke](https://www.luke.fi/); [Kuluttajaliitto](https://www.kuluttajaliitto.fi/).

**Hyvää ruokaa. Ilmaiseksi.**

**Critical UX for open, no-reservation distribution**: Show prominent warning **Ei varausta – saatavuus epävarma.** Latest availability reports: **paljon / vähän / loppu**, explicitly timestamped. Supporting texts: **Ruokaa voi olla jäljellä, mutta saatavuutta ei voida taata.** / **Tarkista noutoaika ennen lähtöä.**

Product spec reminders: a **fresh photo is mandatory** for an open distribution; start and end times are mandatory; pickup users can rapidly report amount left and quality feedback. Food bags support selectable category chips (multi-select), optional photo/description, content is not guaranteed. Ratings have four criteria; do not confuse store/reliability/pickup predictability with quality. Push filters: favorite stores, individual stores, nearby, selected towns.

## Content architecture and publication strategy

- Store localized reusable UI strings separate from **country fact records** (value, indicator definition, edible/inedible scope, reference year, source URL, last-verified date, approval status) and **campaign copy** (target country, locale, placement, version, fact ID, CTA).
- Manage centrally / remote-configure copy and banners so mobile app releases are not needed for translations or seasonal campaigns. Do not add paid infrastructure.
- A/B test three message frames: saving money/free food, positive environmental impact, merchant convenience.
- Show statistics primarily in acquisition campaigns, initial onboarding, merchant acquisition and dedicated impact page, **not** as interruptive data on every app open.
- Personalize campaign by selected country and language, with EU/world fallbacks.
- Only show verified FreeToGo-specific impact metrics based on real fulfilled pickups and known quantities; do not claim all distributed food was certainly rescued from disposal.
- No fees and no commissions at launch; avoid implying long-term pricing is irrevocably fixed.
- Keep English/Finnish source copy editable and review all localized marketing with native speakers.

- [ ] Confirm Eurostat dataset version, reference year, unit and country-by-country figures against official download.
- [ ] Confirm Luke 2023–2024 edible-vs-total distinctions and household share.
- [ ] Confirm UNEP actual Food Waste Index Report 2024 citation and global-meals wording.
- [ ] Avoid conflating EU (27 members) and geographically wider Europe.
- [ ] Validate 14 localized language variants, cultural tone and legal language.
- [ ] Include small readable source/footnote links on every numerical banner and relevant landing page.
- [ ] Ensure all listings truthfully distinguish **reserved bag** from **open distribution**.
- [ ] Do not send deceptive availability notifications when food is gone.
